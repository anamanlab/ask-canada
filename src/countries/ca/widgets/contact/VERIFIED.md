- CRA "Recognize a scam" https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/scams-fraud/recognize-scam.html [2026-08-06]:
  "Recovery room scams": messages target people who already lost money, promising to recover it.
  Re-checked 2026-09-30 ("Warning signs of a scam"): the CRA will not send refunds by e-transfer or text; will
  not provide or accept cryptocurrency; will not demand or pressure immediate payment by Interac e-transfer,
  cryptocurrencies, prepaid credit cards or gift cards; will not threaten to deport or arrest you or put you in
  prison; will not use aggressive or threatening language; will not charge a fee to speak with an agent; will not
  ask for personal or financial information in a voicemail or email. On a REAL call the CRA may ask for your
  SIN to verify identity, so the card never says "the CRA never asks for your SIN". "Phone number and website
  spoofing are common." "When in doubt, verify": check with the CRA through your CRA account or by contacting
  it directly (not a number from the message).
  antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca is not on the pack's source allowlist, so cited sources are the
  canada.ca / competition-bureau.canada.ca pages above (same facts); CAFC pages stay as plain action links.
