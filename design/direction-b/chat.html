You asked: How do I renew my passport? It expires in March.

Ask Canada·4 official sources

Good news: you can most likely renew your passport instead of applying for a new one. It’s simpler — no guarantor and no proof of citizenship.1

Because it expires in March, you’re inside the 6-month window to renew online — as long as it expires on or before March 29. Online renewal takes about 20 business days, plus up to 5 days for delivery.2

One thing to know: your current passport is cancelled as soon as you apply online. If you’re travelling in the next few weeks, apply in person instead.2 I’ve set up a plan from what you told me:
