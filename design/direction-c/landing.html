How it works

From question
to done.

No department names to learn, no forms to hunt for. Say what’s going on in your life, and Ask Canada finds the service, explains it, and shows you the next step.

  1. STEP 1

    Ask the way you’d ask a person.

    Type it, say it, or attach the letter you got from the CRA. English or French — switch any time.

    “I just lost my job. What now?”VOICE
    Quand dois-je produire ma déclaration?FR

  2. STEP 2

    Get a clear answer, with its source.

    Short sentences, real numbers, no jargon. Every fact links to the canada.ca page it came from.

    Service standard for a passport submitted at a passport office: 10 business days. By mail or online: 20 business days, plus mailing time.

    Finish on the official site.

Privacy

Private by design. Not by policy.

Ask about your health, your money or your status without leaving a trail. The safest data is the data we never keep.

No account needed

Open it and ask. There is nothing to sign up for.

No tracking cookies

No ad pixels, no profiles, no following you around the web.

Conversations aren’t stored

Your questions are answered, then gone from our servers.

Your plans stay on your device

Checklists and reminders you save live in your browser only.

Never asks for your numbers
