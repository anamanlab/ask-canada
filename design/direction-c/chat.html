How do I renew my passport? It expires in March.
Ask Canada 3 official sources

Good news: you can renew — and with a March expiry, you can do it online.

Renewing is simpler than applying for a new passport. You don’t need a guarantor or proof of citizenship.1

Online renewal opens once your passport is within 6 months of expiring — for a March expiry, that’s now, as long as it expires by March 29. Two things to know first:

  • Applying online cancels your current passport right away. Don’t apply online if you’re travelling in the next 20 business days.1
  • Standard processing is 20 business days plus mailing — or 10 business days if you apply in person at a passport office.2
