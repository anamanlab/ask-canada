How do I renew my passport? It expires in March.

Ask Canada · from 3 official pages

You can most likely renew — and with a March expiry, you can probably do it online.

Renewing is simpler than applying for a new passport: no guarantor, no proof of citizenship and no extra ID.1

Online renewal is open once your passport is within 6 months of expiring, so a March expiry qualifies now or within days. It takes up to 20 business days plus mailing.2

One catch: your current passport is cancelled as soon as you apply online. If you’re travelling in the next 20 business days, apply in person instead.
